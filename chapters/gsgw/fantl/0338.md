# Chapter 337 - Sekwang Special City Disaster, III

Why was Agent Bronze... standing there as a teenager in a Sekwang High uniform?

“...”

The sheer absurdity of the situation nearly made my mind go blank, but I immediately collected myself and started assessing what was happening.

Agent Bronze looked slightly younger than his high school appearance I had seen in the ‘Within the Shadow of Darkness’ ghost story.

And it wasn’t just the uniform that was different.

His expression and aura clearly showed his youth.

“Excuse me…”

He didn’t recognize me.

‘...Has he been assimilated into the Sekwang Special City ghost story?’

Just like me, he seemed to have been ‘placed’ here as a suitable citizen of Sekwang Special City on May 4th.

...Wait a minute.

If all my companions who fell in here were in this state, then how on earth was I supposed to get them out?

How could I convince them?

The relief of finding a companion was brief, immediately replaced by another urgent dilemma.

I desperately racked my brain. Meanwhile, the high school version of Ryu Jaekwan caught his breath and chose his words carefully.

“The bicycle from before... Are you all right?”

“I’m fine.”

I looked at his face, which was still so youthful.

His expression was a mix of bewilderment and concern, but he was remarkably calm for someone his age who had just witnessed something straight out of a ghost story happen in reality.

‘...Agent Bronze did say he was first caught up in a ghost story and rescued by the Disaster Management Bureau when he was a student.’

Could he have been reverted to a point even before that? No, what mattered right now was figuring out how to get him to come with me.

I pretended to look at the uniform of the student, who had no name tag, and asked.

“More importantly... are you a student?”

“Yes.”

“Is your destination by any chance Sekwang High School? That looks like their uniform.”

“It is, but...”

“That’s great. I’m heading that way too.”

I approached the public bike rack and started smashing the bike locks with a heavy stone.

“...!”

“It’ll be difficult to rent a public bike through the app now. This will be faster than trying in vain.”

“...”

“On your way here, did you see anyone swelling up? Or get a Disaster Alert?”

“...Yes.”

“From now on, only listen for the alarm and never check the Disaster Alert itself. If you see people walking together in a crowd on the street, stay far away from them.”

By then, I had completely broken one bike lock, but I didn’t stop and moved on to the next one.

I pulled one out and offered it to the high school student.

“Do you know how to ride a bike?”

The high school version of Ryu Jaekwan looked at me with trembling eyes, then nodded firmly.

I was leading the way, riding my bike.

Behind me, the high school student Ryu Jaekwan followed on the public bike I had given him.

Seeing him keep up with clenched teeth, it seemed Agent Bronze’s cycling skills had been exceptional from the very beginning...

I skillfully dodged the Disaster Alerts and screams coming from all over, continuously taking detours as I rode toward Sekwang Technical High School.

But those warning signals would soon disappear.

…Once no survivors were left in the area, the alerts would naturally stop sounding.

‘I have to move fast.’

My mind was racing. As I planned our route... I spotted something.

A convenience store.

“Wait a moment.”

“Yes?”

Making sure Ryu Jaekwan’s movements weren’t faltering, I stopped in front of the convenience store.

I quickly opened the door, went inside, and swept up various items.

‘Prioritize food.’

But I didn’t take too much. Mobility was essential.

After some thought, I grabbed two of the sturdiest-looking large umbrellas and a tool kit.

Then, I spoke to the part-timer at the counter, who had been glancing at a book with earphones in throughout the entire transaction.

“You need to get out of here. Quickly.”

“Huh?”

“There was a biochemical terror attack near City Hall, and it’s spreading. Get to the subway station as fast as you can.”

“!!”

Only then did the part-timer turn on his phone to check the news. His face went pale as he began scrolling through his screen frantically.

As I left, I caught a glimpse of the part-timer grabbing items from the convenience store, just like I had...

Then I looked at the high school student Ryu Jaekwan, who was watching me awkwardly while holding his bike.

“Open your bag.”

“Huh?”

I put two small bottles of water and several calorie bars into Ryu Jaekwan’s school bag. Then I handed him an umbrella and had him strap it to the side of his bike.

“We don’t know what the situation will be like from now on, so it’s best to be prepared. Use the umbrella to push things away in an emergency.”

“Th-thank you.”

Ryu Jaekwan took the bag but hesitated before speaking.

“I-I’ll pay you for it.”

“I’m not about to take money from a student in an emergency like this. Get on your bike. We’re heading to Sekwang High, quickly.”

“But... okay.”

Ryu Jaekwan looked like he had a lot to say and many questions, but knowing the gravity of the situation, he wiped his sweat and grabbed his bike again.

But in the end, he asked.

“How... do you know all this so well?”

“...I’m used to it.”

“Huh?”

I quickly changed the subject.

“More importantly, school hours have already passed. Why are you heading to school only now?”

“I-I had to go to the bank, so I was a little late.”

“Have you contacted your family?”

“...It’s fine.”

From that euphemistic reply, I realized that Ryu Jaekwan’s backstory here was likely similar to reality.
