# Chapter 337 - Sekwang Special City Disaster, III

Why was Agent Bronze standing in front of me as a Sekwang High teenager?

“...”

The situation was so absurd that my thoughts almost stalled. I forced them back into order and examined what I was seeing.

He looked even younger than the student version I had encountered inside the ‘Within the Shadow of Darkness’ ghost story.

The uniform was only the most obvious change.

His face and the atmosphere around him both carried an unmistakably adolescent quality.

“Um...”

He didn’t recognize me.

‘...Was he absorbed into the ghost story of Sekwang Special City?’

Like me, he appeared to have been assigned a role as an ordinary citizen living through May 4th.

...Wait a minute.

If everyone who had fallen here with me had been rewritten this way, how could I possibly take them back out?

Where would I even begin convincing them that this world was false?

Finding an ally brought me relief for only a moment before a more pressing problem took its place.

I searched frantically for an answer. Beside me, the young Ryu Jaekwan recovered his breath and spoke with hesitation.

“That bicycle incident... are you hurt?”

“I’m fine.”

I studied his young face.

He looked confused and worried, yet he remained composed for a student who had just watched a ghost-story nightmare unfold in front of him.

‘...Agent Bronze once told me that the Bureau rescued him from his first ghost story when he was still a student.’

‘Could this be a point in his life even earlier than that?’ No. First, I had to make him leave with me.

I glanced at the nameless uniform as though I were only trying to identify his school.

“You’re a student, right?”

“Yes.”

“Are you heading to Sekwang Technical High? Your uniform looks like theirs.”

“It is, but...”

“Good. I’m going in the same direction.”

I went to the shared-bike rack and raised a chunk of stone against one of the locks.

“...!”

“The app probably won’t be useful anymore. Breaking this is quicker.”

“...”

“Did you see anyone’s body start to swell on the way here? Did your phone receive an alert?”

“...Yes.”

“Listen for the warning tone, but don’t read the message. And if a large group is walking together, avoid them completely.”

The first lock gave way. I immediately began working on the next.

I freed a bicycle and pushed it toward him.

“Can you ride?”

Ryu Jaekwan looked at me with unsteady eyes, then gave a decisive nod.

I took the lead on my bicycle.

The young Ryu Jaekwan followed close behind on the bike I had taken for him.

He held on despite the strain, which suggested that Agent Bronze had always been a capable rider...

I threaded through the streets toward Sekwang Technical High, avoiding the screams and warning tones by taking one detour after another.

Those warnings would not continue forever.

...When no living people remained nearby, there would be no phones left to ring.

‘I have to move fast.’

My thoughts raced as I mapped out our route. Then I noticed a storefront.

A convenience store stood ahead.

“Hold on.”

“What is it?”

I checked that Ryu Jaekwan was still steady, then braked outside the store.

I rushed in and gathered whatever looked useful.

‘Food first.’

I kept the load light. We needed to stay mobile.

After a moment’s consideration, I took two sturdy umbrellas and a toolkit.

The part-timer at the register had been reading with earphones in while I moved around the shop. I addressed him before leaving.

“Leave this place immediately.”

“Huh?”

“A biochemical attack began near City Hall and it’s spreading. Reach the subway before it gets here.”

“!!”

Only then did the part-timer reach for his phone. His face drained of color as he searched the news, scrolling faster and faster.

As I stepped out, I saw him stuffing supplies into a bag, copying my example...

I turned to Ryu Jaekwan, who was gripping his bicycle and watching me uncertainly.

“Give me your bag.”

“Huh?”

I placed two small water bottles and a few energy bars inside his schoolbag, then passed him an umbrella to secure to the bicycle.

“We don’t know what comes next. Keep these with you, and use the umbrella to shove anything away if you’re cornered.”

“Th-thank you.”

Ryu Jaekwan took the bag but hesitated before speaking.

“I-I can pay you back.”

“I’m not taking money from a student during an emergency. Get on. We need to reach Sekwang High fast.”

“But... okay.”

Ryu Jaekwan clearly had questions, but the seriousness of the situation kept him from asking them. He wiped his forehead and took hold of the handlebars.

After a while, he spoke.

“How do you know what to do?”

“...Experience.”

“Huh?”

I quickly changed the subject.

“More importantly, school has already started. Why are you only going there now?”

“I-I had to stop at the bank. That’s why I’m late.”

“Have you contacted your family?”

“...It’s fine.”

The evasive answer suggested that this version of Ryu Jaekwan carried the same history as the real one.

Talismans filled the cracks around every classroom door and window, stirring an old, complicated feeling in me.

‘So this was the form Team Leader Hong’s barrier took...’

The children were being kept asleep while the disaster day looped around them.

That suspended state was likely counted as ‘death,’ much like the Snow White Mountain Apples I had consumed. It separated them from the Annihilation-sanctioned Disaster carried by the alerts.

So the school doors had to remain closed.

They probably wouldn’t have opened easily anyway.

Ryu Jaekwan, who belonged to this school, looked horrified by his classmates lying motionless in rows along the corridor.

“What is this? What on earth...?”

“That talisman.”

“...”

For an instant, an unfamiliar glimmer passed through Ryu Jaekwan’s eyes as he followed the design.

Then worry and confusion covered it, leaving only the face of an ordinary teenager.

When he faced me again, suspicion tightened his expression.

“I don’t know what you’re talking about.”

“...”

Of course, a few words would not be enough to wake him. I had needed more than six hundred repetitions to escape this place myself.

From his perspective, I was simply a suspicious stranger babbling about charms during an emergency.

‘I can’t afford to be impatient.’

In that case...

“There’s no need to worry.”

I smiled at the teenage Ryu Jaekwan.

“They’re protective talismans. As long as they remain attached, the danger outside cannot reach this school.”

An expression that seemed to ask, ‘How could you possibly know that?’ crossed his face, but his wariness gradually shifted toward curiosity.

“Your friends will be safe. We should concentrate on getting out.”

“...What about you, sir? You said you came here to find someone.”

Right. Ho Yuwon.

But another question occurred to me.

If the building was sealed and the students were unconscious, how had the Counselor managed to leave?
